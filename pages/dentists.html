Bachelor of Dentistry · Otago University, 1983

Graeme brings broad professional experience and more than three decades of caring for patients in Lismore.

Before establishing his long connection with the local community, Graeme practised in Casino, Ballina and England. His approach combines established clinical experience with an ongoing interest in professional education and family-friendly dental care.
