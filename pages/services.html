01

Routine care

Check-ups & preventive care

Regular examinations and professional care can help identify changes early and support healthier teeth and gums over time.

  • Routine dental examinations
  • Professional cleans and hygiene support
  • Preventive advice tailored to your needs
  • Ongoing maintenance planning

02

Repair and restore

Fillings & restorations

Tooth-coloured restorations can repair areas affected by decay, wear or damage while supporting comfortable, natural function.

  • Assessment of decay, wear and damage
  • Natural-looking tooth-coloured fillings
  • Repair of chipped teeth where appropriate
  • Replacement of unsuitable restorations

03

Custom-made care

Crowns & inlays/onlays

Custom restorations may be recommended when a tooth needs more strength and support than a direct filling can provide.

  • Custom crowns for weakened teeth
  • Inlays and onlays where appropriate
  • Restoration of shape and natural function
  • Careful shade and material selection
Book a check-up ↗
04

Preserving natural teeth

Root canal treatment

Root canal treatment may help relieve discomfort and preserve a natural tooth affected by infection or inflammation.

  • Assessment of symptoms and the affected tooth
  • Cleaning and sealing of the canal system
  • Comfort-focused treatment planning
  • Planning for the tooth's final restoration

05

Personalised smile care

Cosmetic dentistry

Cosmetic care begins with understanding your goals and assessing which options are suitable for your oral health.

  • Professional teeth-whitening options
  • Cosmetic tooth-coloured restorations
  • Assessment of shape, shade and balance
  • Planning focused on natural-looking results

06

Assessment and coordination

Extractions & referrals

When a tooth cannot be predictably restored, extraction may be discussed and more complex care can be coordinated with trusted specialists.

  • Assessment of damaged or problematic teeth
  • Appropriate extractions within the practice
  • Discussion of replacement options
  • Referral for more complex treatment

Common questions

Before choosing a service.

Your first step does not need to be a final treatment decision. An assessment gives your dentist the information needed to explain what may be suitable for you.

Ask reception a question ↗
How do I know which appointment to book?

Choose a check-up for routine care. For pain, a broken tooth or another specific concern, call reception so the team can help select an appropriate appointment type.

Will treatment options be explained first?

Yes. Your dentist will explain the findings, suitable options and expected next steps before treatment proceeds.

Can I ask about costs before deciding?

Treatment recommendations and expected costs can be discussed as part of your planning, allowing you to make an informed decision.

What happens if I need specialist care?

When care falls outside the appropriate scope of the practice, your dentist can explain the reason for referral and coordinate with a trusted specialist.

Are new patients welcome?

Yes. New patients can book online or contact reception for help selecting a suitable first appointment.
