Payment options

Choose the approach that fits your situation.

Payment and claiming options depend on your treatment, eligibility and each provider’s conditions. Reception can explain what is currently available.

01

Standard payment

Card payment

Pay your appointment fee at reception using an accepted card payment method.

When
Generally after your appointment
02

Health-fund claiming

HICAPS

Eligible private health-fund claims may be processed at reception, with any remaining gap paid after claiming.

Bring
Your current health-fund card
03

External provider

Afterpay

Ask reception whether Afterpay is currently available for your planned treatment.

Approval
Provider conditions apply
04

Dental finance

Fund My Dental

An external finance option that may help eligible patients manage treatment costs.

Application
Provider approval required
05

Treatment finance

TLC

TLC may provide treatment finance for eligible patients, subject to its assessment and repayment conditions.

Repayment
Terms are managed by TLC

Helms & White Dental does not determine external-provider approvals, credit decisions, fees or repayment terms. Confirm current conditions directly with the relevant provider.

Eligibility-based programs

Supported care for eligible patients.

Government programs have their own eligibility, benefit and treatment conditions. Reception can help confirm what information you need before booking.

CDBS

For eligible children

Child Dental Benefits Schedule

Confirm eligibility, remaining benefits and whether the planned treatment is covered before the appointment.

DVA

For eligible veterans

Department of Veterans’ Affairs

Bring the required details and ask reception to confirm the current coverage and approval requirements.

Before you decide

The right time to ask questions is before treatment begins.

A treatment plan should help you understand what is recommended, why it may be needed and how the care may be staged.

01

Ask for the expected fee

Costs can be discussed as part of treatment planning before you choose how to proceed.

02

Confirm what may be claimable

Health-fund benefits vary, so your fund remains the best source for exact benefit information.

03

Discuss timing and stages

Some treatment may be completed in stages when clinically appropriate and agreed with your dentist.
