Our history

A legacy built one patient at a time.

The tools and techniques of dentistry have changed enormously, but the qualities that matter most remain the same: trust, consistency and genuine care for the people we serve.

  1. 01

    1935 · The beginning

    A dental practice begins serving Lismore.

    The practice was established with a commitment to dependable local dental care and strong patient relationships.

  2. 02

    Across generations

    Trust grows through continuity and community.

    Families returned over the years, often introducing children and grandchildren to the same familiar practice.

  3. 03

    Raising the standard

    Accreditation reinforces safe, consistent care.

    Helms & White became the second dental practice in New South Wales to achieve practice accreditation.

  4. 04

    Today · Looking ahead

    Local experience meets modern dentistry.

    Dr Graeme Helms and Dr Brendan White continue the practice's story with contemporary systems and patient-focused care.

Continuing the story

Proud of our history. Focused on your future.

Helms & White remains an independent local practice with a strong connection to the community it serves.

Our team combines established clinical experience with modern technology and ongoing professional development. The result is a practice that feels familiar and personal while continuing to evolve.

  • Family dental care for children, adults and older patients
  • Preventive, restorative and cosmetic treatment options
  • Modern digital systems supporting accurate treatment planning
  • Coordination with trusted specialists when appropriate
